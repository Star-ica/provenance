How the audit floor works

Three stages stand between a mint and a sale. None can be skipped.

01 — Submit

List the work

Creator submits the file, edition details and a reserve price. The listing is locked pending review — it cannot be edited once audit begins.

02 — Review

Independent audit

A reviewer checks originality, metadata integrity and edition history before marking the piece Verified or Rejected on-chain.

03 — Bid

Open bidding

Verified works go live for a fixed window. Every bid is escrowed; outbid bidders can withdraw instantly. Highest bid at close wins the piece.
